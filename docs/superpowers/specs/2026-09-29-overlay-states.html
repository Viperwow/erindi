Erindi overlay: approved states

Approved 2026-09-29. The strip at the bottom shows the microphone only. The bottom row shows running work on the left (pulsing dot) and the microphone on the right; with nothing running the microphone takes the left slot; global errors not tied to a phrase go there too. Phrase text is always neutral. A thin rail on the left of each phrase shows its state: amber — the agent works on it, blue — being spoken, violet — being transcribed, dashed grey — queued, green — done, red — error, light grey — cancelled (text struck through). Finished and failed phrases always have a tooltip: outcome or reason, a divider, the full text. Anything cut off opens in a tooltip; other lines do not react to the mouse. Icons and dots centre on the capital letters of their line.
