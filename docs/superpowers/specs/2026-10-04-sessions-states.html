Rails: one per phrase

Waiting

How are the schools in Montreuil

Transcribingthe phrase is finalised or checked for a command; purple
And what about Lyon?
Queuedwaits for the agent to finish; dashed grey
Now show it in the summary
Runningthe agent works or answers; amber
Add a rent field to the form
Doneanswered; soft green, text dims
Run the release script
Failedthe agent failed; red
Never mind
Cancelledyou cancelled it; faint grey, struck through

Marks: one per session, left of its title

Waitingthe active session waits for your voice; hollow dark blue circle, still
Listeningyou are talking to it; blue dot, pulsing
Transcribingyour phrase is being finalised; purple triangle pointing down, pulsing
Answeringits agent works or answers; amber pentagon, turning clockwise
Idlenothing is happening; grey dot, still

Queued and cancelled show only on the phrase rail: a queued phrase means the session is already busy, and a cancel stops a phrase, not the session.
